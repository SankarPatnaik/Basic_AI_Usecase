EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - SAMPLE DATA

Annual Leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of annual leave per calendar year. Planned leave should normally be requested at least five working days in advance. A maximum of 10 unused annual-leave days may be carried into the next calendar year.

Sick Leave
An employee who is unwell should notify the manager as early as possible. Advance approval is not required for unexpected illness. A medical certificate may be requested when the absence lasts more than three consecutive working days.

Remote Work
Employees working remotely must be available during agreed business hours. Company information must only be accessed through approved devices and approved secure connections.

Information Security
Passwords must not be shared. Confidential documents must not be uploaded to unapproved public services. Suspected security incidents must be reported immediately to the service desk or information-security team.
